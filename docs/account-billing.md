# Account & Billing

Manage your BetterBlog profile and subscription from **Account** in the dashboard sidebar.

---

## Plans

All plans include the full feature set. The only difference is how many Squarespace blogs you can connect:

| Plan | Blogs |
|---|---|
| **Essentials** | 1 |
| **Professional** | 3 |
| **Publication** | Unlimited |

Monthly and annual billing are available. Annual billing is discounted. You can change or cancel from Account at any time.

---

## Starting a free trial

Every new signup includes a **7-day free trial**. Your card is not charged until the trial ends. Choose a plan at checkout (Stripe). BetterBlog never stores card numbers.

If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged.

---

## Logging in with a magic link

BetterBlog does not use passwords.

1. Go to the login page and enter the email on your account.
2. Open the email and click the magic link.
3. You are signed in for 30 days.

Magic links expire after **24 hours**. Requesting a new link invalidates earlier ones. Check spam if the email does not arrive. There is no “forgot password” flow.

Your **email address cannot be changed** in the dashboard. Contact the team from Support if you need the account moved to a different email. You can update your **Full Name** on the Account page and click **Save Changes**.

BetterBlog does not offer teammate seats or a Studio plan. One login per account.

---

## Upgrading or changing your plan

On Account, under **Current Plan**, click **Change Plan**. That opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can switch plans and billing cadence.

If you cannot add another blog, you have reached the plan limit (1 on Essentials, 3 on Professional). Upgrade from Account.

---

## Updating billing information

Click **Update Payment Method** on the Account page. That opens the Stripe billing portal to change the card on file. Invoices and receipt history are in the Stripe portal (they are also emailed each billing cycle). There is no separate Invoice History screen inside BetterBlog.

---

## Canceling your subscription

Click **Cancel Subscription** and confirm. Cancellation is scheduled for the **end of the current billing period** — it is not immediate. You keep full access until that date, shown on the Account page.

If you canceled by mistake, click **Restore Subscription** before the period ends.

---

## What happens when a subscription expires

After the period ends (or a past-due invoice is not paid), BetterBlog stops rendering on your live blog. Your Squarespace content is unchanged. Customization, analytics, and leads remain in your BetterBlog account. Re-subscribe from Account / Stripe to restore the overlay.

Removing the snippet from Squarespace Code Injection also reverts the live blog immediately, even if the subscription is still active. See [Installation](installation.md).
