# Post Settings

The Post tab in **Customize Blog** controls how individual blog posts look. Open Customize Blog, select **Post** at the top of the panel, then adjust the sections below. Click **Save** to apply changes to your live posts.

Settings a template owns are hidden. If a control is missing, your current post template is making that decision. See [Templates](templates.md).

---

## Reader Experience

**Show Date** — Shows the publish date in the post header.

**Show Reading Time** — Shows estimated reading time.

**Show Author(s)** — Shows author name(s) in post meta. Turning this on reveals author management.

**Progress Bar** — A reading progress indicator that fills as the reader scrolls the post. Turn it on or off here. Position (top of the viewport), thickness, and color use BetterBlog defaults.

---

## Post header

**Post Header** controls the top of the post: featured image, title, and meta.

**Show featured image** — Displays the post’s featured image in the header. Publisher, Writer, and Story lock this.

**Image position** — Full bleed, left of post info, right of post info, or below post info. Some templates limit this to left/right only (Reporter, Story). Publisher and Feature lock position.

**Caption (if exists)** — Shows the image caption when Squarespace has one. Hidden on Writer.

**Vertical text placement** — Top, Center, or Bottom for title overlay on full-bleed images.

**Post info alignment** — Left, Center, or Right for the title and meta.

**Vertical post info alignment** — Top, Center, or Bottom when the image sits beside the post info.

**Show breadcrumbs** — A trail above the post title (typically Home → collection → post).

**Show Tags & Categories** — Checkboxes for **Tags** and **Categories**, shown after breadcrumbs and before the title. Locked on all current post templates.

**Show post byline** — A lead line from the excerpt, after the title and before author and date. Publisher locks this.

**Show decorative accent line** — A horizontal divider in the header. Placement varies by template. Publisher and Feature lock this.

**Social sharing links** — Share buttons in the post header. Choose **All** or specific platforms: Facebook, X, Email, Reddit, LinkedIn, Pinterest, WhatsApp.

**Background color** — Available on the Story template for the full-width header zone.

## Table of Contents

The table of contents is generated from heading blocks in the post (Heading 2 and Heading 3). Plain bold text is not picked up — use actual heading blocks in Squarespace.

**Location** — Left Sidebar or Right Sidebar.

**Style** — Numbered, Connected dots, or Bookmark.

The TOC section is hidden on the Story template and whenever the template locks it. You cannot place it in a sidebar the template has locked.

If entries are missing, check that the post uses heading blocks, not just larger or bolded body text.

---

## Sidebars

A sidebar appears when you add modules to **Left Sidebar** or **Right Sidebar**. On the Post tab you can set **Sticky (move with scroll)** and **Module order**.

Post sidebar modules: Table of Contents, Author Profiles, Popular Posts, Related Posts, Filter by Category, Filter by Tags & Categories, Email Capture, Lead Magnet.

Reporter, Writer, Feature, and Story lock the left sidebar. Writer, Feature, and Story also lock the right sidebar. If the section is missing, the template does not use that sidebar.

If a sticky sidebar overlaps your Squarespace header, turn sticky off. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

---

## Social sharing

On the Post tab, sharing is controlled in **Post Header → Social sharing links** (see above). Collection-page sharing is locked on every collection template.

---

## Footer modules

**Footer Content** sits below the post body (and comments, when comments are shown).

**Side margins** — **Post Body** (align with the text column) or **Full Screen** (site default margins). Hidden on Writer.

**Add module…** — **Related Posts**, **Author Profiles**, **Previous/Next Article**, **Email Capture**, **Lead Magnet**. Drag to reorder; remove a module to turn it off.

**Previous/Next Article** has no separate settings section — add it from Footer Content.

**Related Posts**, **Author Profiles**, **Email Capture**, and **Lead Magnet** also have location and copy controls under Navigation & Discovery. Related Posts can live in a sidebar or the footer.

---

## Author profiles

Authors are managed from **Show Author(s)** on either the Collection or Post tab.

**Default Author(s)** — Site-wide defaults used when a post has no override. Use **Add author…** or **Add New Author**.

**Post Author(s)** — Shown when a post is selected in the preview. Adding or removing authors here overrides the defaults for that post only.

**Add New Author** / **Edit Author** fields:

- **Name** (required)
- Photo
- **Short bio** (max 200 characters) — used in sidebars
- **Longer bio** (max 1000 characters) — used in the footer
- **Email**
- Optional social links: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn
- **Default author** checkbox — also add to site defaults

**Edit Author Profiles** opens the editor for an author already on the current list. Removing a chip from Default Author(s) or Post Author(s) removes that author from the list; it does not delete the author record.

---

## Comments

Comment settings live on the Post tab under **Comments**, and can also be changed on the **Comments** dashboard page. See [Comments](comments.md).

---

## Paywall

Paywall copy is not nested under Collection or Post. If the blog is membership-gated, use **Paywall Settings** below **Clear all settings**. Those values apply to both views. See [Paywall & Membership](paywall-membership.md).

---

## Saving and preview

Click **Save** to publish layout changes. Comments toggles on the Comments page save immediately. Preview limitations on `.squarespace.com` domains are covered in [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
